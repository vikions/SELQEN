# SELQEN
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People reviewing Robinhood Stock Token trades inside existing dApps.

## Product Purpose
A compact browser safety layer which brings canonical identity, corporate actions and valuation evidence into the trade review.

## Brand Commitments
SELQEN. Know what you're signing. Browser safety layer for Robinhood Stock Tokens.
Minimal, serious, high contrast financial review UI, with clear scope and evidence beside the trade.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Chrome MV3 side panel; read-only checks; no wallet custody, signing, analytics or secrets. No universal dApp compatibility. Fixtures must be unmistakable. No green transaction verdict when critical evidence or intent coverage is missing.

## Evidence on Hand
Mainnet MVP v0.1.0. See docs/coverage.md for dated evidence: September 28 extension-worker reads of AAPL issuer/contract data and AAPL/USD plus USDG/USD feeds; controlled browser tests of amount observation and provider forwarding. On October 4, 41 tests, typecheck and build passed. User-driven mainnet/MetaMask acceptance remains pending.

## Open Decisions
Hackathon eligibility of an extension without a new deployed contract. Morpho and exact signature inspection remain later scope unless independently validated.
