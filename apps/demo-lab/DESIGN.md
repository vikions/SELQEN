# SELQEN public website

## Overview
Persuade: a concise extension landing page. Product installation is primary, a labelled interactive preview is proof, and the full lab stays at #lab. User requested a replacement for the dense laboratory homepage: less information, more style. Use a dark ink canvas, oversized editorial type and acid-green controls; a white extension panel is the focal contrast. Reference principles: Pocket Universe shows the extension doing its job; Honey makes installation the primary action. Do not borrow their claims or illustrations.

## Colors
Canvas #101310, foreground #f2f3e9, secondary #a8b0a5, accent #c2f970, divider #30372e. Preview panel uses warm paper #f4f5ed and ink #172019. Amber is reserved for the illustrative warning state.

## Typography
Use the existing locally bundled Geist. Display up to 96px, tight line height, tracking -0.04em at most. Body 16px. Short copy and generous space; no repeated feature cards or decorative statistics.

## Layout
Thin header, asymmetric hero with headline left and browser/extension composition right. One compact product scope line, an installation section and quiet footer. On mobile, headline precedes preview and controls remain at least 44px tall. No horizontal scrolling.

## Components
Solid green primary link; understated text links. The preview switches between two labelled synthetic cases using buttons. The installation instructions use native details. Browser chrome is an illustrative product frame, never a screenshot of a real connected wallet. The full lab and actual extension keep their existing operation-focused styling.

## Motion
One subtle preview entrance; no endless animation. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Focus outlines must be visible on dark and light surfaces.
